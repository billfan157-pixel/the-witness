  [H] to the PR-04 spatial envelopes. Its PBR maps reuse the credited architecture
  sets below; the character rigs reuse the existing project character pipeline.
  These are artistic reconstructions, with `artAccepted: false` pending review.

  by `tools/prepare-q01-sound.cjs`: sea/wind/fire beds, material foley, a bell and

  `audio/q01/procedural-sources.json`.
- `audio/q01/natan-night.mp3` is AI-generated fictional speech through ElevenLabs,
  model `eleven_v4`, using the connected library voice
  “Quang Toan – Deep Vietnamese Storyteller”. Generation
  `GUzMYCIZH2rUu4Scc0sr`, flow `dNgSp1YPc2drSe5XU6T5`.
  Script: screenplay 07, Q01 Beat 07; five Natan sentences, 14.24 s.
  `audio/q01/voice-sources.json` records provenance/hash and the limits of the
  provider's timing alignment. Usage remains subject to the ElevenLabs account
  terms; no CC0 or human-actor claim. Additional voice production was unavailable;
  this file does not establish full dialogue coverage or listening approval.
- `audio/q01/vieneu/**` and `audio/q01/vieneu-sources.json` are local experimental
  Vietnamese speech assets [H] synthesized via **VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo**
  (`pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS-v3-Turbo`, sha `61b85e3d937fbbacb387714180e8182823512523`)

  (`ceff0d0749bfb3fa2d61149794ec6feef0d1e1ae`), both **Apache-2.0**, executed
  locally on CPU ONNX Runtime (`fp32`) by `tools/vieneu-tts.cjs` +
  `tools/vieneu-tts-worker.py` + `tools/vieneu-cast.json`. Zero external voice
  cloning (`allowVoiceCloning: false`; only curated built-in preset voices used:
  Natan Beat 07 `Thiền Tâm Đức` & `Thiện Minh`, Titus `Hải Đăng`, Yosef `Xuân Vĩnh`,
  Rina `Ngọc Linh`; 28 runtime clips + 2 Natan Beat 07 comparison reels against

  are shipped; all VieNeu takes retain `reviewStatus: "generated-awaiting-human-audio-review"`

## World upgrade 2026-11-10 (environment & world building)

- `terrain/heightmaps/{galilee,jerusalem,caesarea}.{r16,png}` are project-authored
  16-bit heightmaps [H]: gradient-noise macro shape plus droplet hydraulic
  erosion and thermal talus weathering, simulated by
  `tools/prepare-heightmaps.cjs` (the same simulation class Gaea/World Machine
  run). They are not exports from those applications; real 16-bit RAW exports
  can replace them via `--external`. Parameters and seeds: `terrain/heightmaps/sources.json`.
- `environment-kit/arch/*.glb` modular ashlar wall and Roman column modules are
  project-authored geometry [H] from `witness-modular-kit.js`, exported by
  `tools/environment-kit/build-arch-kit.mjs` with Meshopt geometry compression
  and KTX2/Basis Universal textures (ETC1S albedo/ORM, UASTC normal). Limestone
  PBR maps derive from the CC0 Poly Haven study listed below. Vertex colour
  channels carry a weathering mask (R cavity dirt, G arris wear, B moisture/moss),
  not a baked albedo tint.
- Water, terrain POM and flora wind shaders are project-authored GLSL [H] in
  `vendor/adventure-visuals.js`, `vendor/witness-graphics.js` and
  `witness-ambience.js`; the planar-reflection matrix math follows the Three.js
  Reflector example (MIT, `THREE-LICENSE.txt`).

(Pillow required). No CDN is needed during gameplay or in the standalone build.

## Environment kit pipeline study

`CAE-WALL-PBR-02` uses the existing CC0 Poly Haven
[Coral Ground 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/coral_ground_02) atlas tile.
Checked 2026-11-09. Cropped albedo/normal/data maps, generated UVs, resized
textures and encoded KTX2; terrain height was removed from ORM metallic B.
The wall geometry is project-authored [H]. This is a porous limestone study,
not an accepted reconstruction of Caesarea kurkar.

`vendor/witness-loaders.js` bundles r187 Three GLTFLoader/KTX2Loader addons
under the existing Three MIT license. Meshoptimizer 1.3.0 uses MIT;
`vendor/basis/MESHOPT-LICENSE` contains its license. Local Basis transcoder
JS/WASM copied from Three r187 is Apache 2.0; LICENSE and NOTICE are kept
in `vendor/basis/`. The build encoder is Basis Universal v2.50 WASI, pinned
by SHA256 in `tools/environment-kit/setup-encoder.mjs`; it is a build tool,
not a runtime download.

The regional reference board uses original project SVG diagrams [H].
External archaeological photographs are linked only, not redistributed.
Research sources, per-image restrictions and reconstruction limits are
recorded in `docs/environment-kit/research/`.

## Architecture material candidates

Four Poly Haven set fetched 2026-11-09 under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license)
as Phase 4 comparison surfaces. They are not loaded by the player runtime and
are not accepted art or archaeological measurements:

- Stone: [Sandstone Blocks 05](https://polyhaven.com/a/sandstone_blocks_05), Rob Tuytel.
- Plaster: [Plastered Wall 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall_04), Rob Tuytel.
- Wood: [Weathered Brown Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/weathered_brown_planks), Dimitrios Savva (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing).
- Floor: [Monastery Stone Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/monastery_stone_floor), Amal Kumar.

Modifications: gamma-aware resize; OpenGL normal renormalized after resize;
linear ORM packed with metalness B set to zero and a recorded roughness clamp;
near-lossless WebP in `architecture/full/` and `architecture/lite/`. Source
URLs, MD5/SHA256, dimensions and per-slot interpretation limits are recorded in
`architecture/sources.json`. Rebuild with
`node tools/environment-kit/prepare-house-materials.mjs` (sharp required).

## Q01 Phase B environment

`assets/architecture/phase-b/` contains CC0 Poly Haven comparison surfaces
from `sandstone_blocks_05` and `stone_wall_03`. The generator, authors,
official source URLs, pinned MD5/SHA-256 values, mortar-free crop quilt,
OpenGL normal processing and output hashes are in its `sources.json`.
These modern scans are artistic comparisons [H], not sampled Caesarea
kurkar or a verified ancient concrete recipe.

The curved quay, plastered store kit, door joinery, high ventilation slots,
paving transitions and contact dressing are original project geometry in
`witness-q01-environment.js`. Their layout is [H]; archaeological limits
are recorded in `docs/environment-kit/research/q01-phase-b-materials.md`.

data produced by `tools/bake-q01-gi.cjs`; `gi-bake.json` records the proxy
geometry, lighting inputs, occlusion checks, precision and limitations.
No external archaeological photograph was copied into these assets.

## Cover

`journey-cover.webp` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, then encoded
to WebP for the title screen.

### Generation prompt

> Use case: historical-scene. Photoreal cinematic landscape cover, wide horizontal composition, for a serious story-driven biblical-era RPG. A lone older Levantine traveler in worn layered indigo and umber linen walking away from camera with a wooden staff on the right third, midground full figure, footpath curling through rocky Upper Mesopotamian hills toward a small tent encampment in a green-ochre valley, late sunset low behind distant ridges, atmospheric depth, weathered limestone, dry grass, little woodsmoke, big textured sky. Dark earth and muted foreground with quiet negative space across the left 47 percent for real UI text. Natural scale, tactile material detail, sober epic film still, warm ivory light. No words, logos, borders, modern elements, church architecture, or weapons.
